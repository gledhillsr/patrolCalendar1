Automatically sync my PatrolCalendar shifts to my personal calendar (one-time setup)

Your shifts can appear in the calendar you already use — Google, Apple or Outlook — and keep themselves up to date as the schedule changes. You only set this up once.

Step 1 — copy your personal link:


Copied

Step 2 — add it to your calendar. Pick whichever you use:

  • Google Calendar — on a computer, not the phone app. In the left column find Other calendars, click +, choose From URL, paste the link, then Add calendar.
  • Apple — iPhone or iPad. Settings → Apps → Calendar → Accounts → Add Account → Other → Add Subscribed Calendar, paste the link, then Next and Save.
  • Apple — Mac. Calendar → File menu → New Calendar Subscription, paste the link, then Subscribe. You can set it to refresh every 5 minutes here.
  • Outlook (outlook.com or Microsoft 365) — go to Calendar, Add calendar → Subscribe from web, paste the link, give it a name, then Import.

It is not instant. How often your calendar checks for changes is up to your calendar, not to us. Apple can check every few minutes; Outlook is usually a few hours; Google can take up to a day and gives you no way to hurry it. If you sign up for a shift and it is not on your phone straight away, nothing is broken.

It only reads. Deleting a shift in your own calendar does not drop the shift — it will simply reappear. Sign up and swap here, as always.

Shifts with no start and end time will not appear. If one of yours is missing, that shift has no times recorded on it; ask a director to add them.

Keep this link private. It needs no password, so anyone you send it to can see when you are working. If it goes astray, replace it — the old one stops working at once, and you paste the new one into your calendar again.
