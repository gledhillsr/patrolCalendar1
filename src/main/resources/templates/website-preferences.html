The button below rewrites times that are already readable into one form — 1600 and 4:30pm both become 16:00 — and fills in a missing start time from a Label that holds one — 4:00P, Vol 10:00AM and 8:30 Paid all give a start time. Your labels are not changed, so the calendar still reads as it does today, and a bare number is never read as a clock time (Train 2 stays a course number). It only touches shift templates, never a day that has already been scheduled, and it leaves alone anything it cannot read rather than guessing. Safe to press more than once.

Templates are what future days are built from. If your season is already laid out on the calendar, those days need the same tidy-up — and they are what a patroller’s calendar actually reads.
